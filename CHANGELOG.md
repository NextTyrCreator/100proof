## [Unreleased]

### Fixed
- The inventory list is plain. Each row is a picture with no name on it, a name of at most 20 characters (the full name opens with the line), one badge (the percentage, or Receipt, Appraised, or Stated), the brand and model or the kind on one line, and the price. The totals, the proof ledger, and the read-twice summary are gone from the top of the list, along with the room dollar totals, the exclamation mark, and the walk and read-twice badges on rows. Those still show inside the opened line. Hide is a quiet outlined minus instead of a solid red circle.
- A price search that timed out was marked as sent and never retried, which left 17 of 21 lines on test 2b priced by the camera's guess. Batches are 8 lines, a failed batch is tried once more, and lines it could not price stay eligible for the final pass. The record now seals as soon as the line pictures are saved, before the slow label reads, so a phone that locks at the end no longer leaves a scan unsealed.
- Finish now on a scan that was cut off first puts back the stills that scan already uploaded (matched by line), then cuts only the missing pictures from the saved clips, loading each clip once, with progress shown. It seals the record after both. Before, it cut one frame at a time from remote video and could take many minutes for a long scan.
- The inventory page leads with the list. Room-change questions, possible repeats, and the before-and-after walk moved into a closed "Scan review" section below the list, and the "Worth a minute" queue is gone. Opening a line shows its confidence, replacement cost, cash value, and where it was priced first, then its picture, then the fields to update.
- After Done, a scan saves a second time once its stills and labels are attached. On a Scan more walk, that second save added every new line again and doubled grouped counts. On a first scan, it could undo a line deleted or hidden while the scan was finishing. The second save now only updates lines that already exist, keeps the person's edits, and never brings back a deleted line.
- Clips 21 to 30 of a 15-minute scan were read as clip 1. Each one counted against the daily review limit, so a long scan could stop with "Today's review limit" near the end. Clip numbers now run to 40 (a full scan plus Scan more).
- Room-change questions now wait in order instead of replacing one another, and a tap no longer clears them. After Done they are asked on the finishing screen. Any still unanswered when the scan saves are kept on the inventory and asked on its page. A yes says how many lines moved, or why none did, adds the room to that clip's label, and logs each moved line. The repeat check at Done can no longer fold a room the person tapped into another room.
- The build record said `ARCHITECTURE.md` was written before kickoff and that the sample rooms were staged. Both were wrong, and were corrected on 2026-11-04 while preparing the repository. The architecture was written on kickoff day. The sample rooms are the house as it stands, filmed during the event.

### Changed
- A close-up of a logo or model plate is that object's brand, model, and any year on the plate. A price or an age said while showing it is stored on that line. The same appliance named twice in one room is one object.
- While a scan is recording, the screen stays awake and shows the camera with Pause. Play and Done appear once it is paused. A line now says whether the walkthrough supported a brand, a likely brand, or no brand, how the replacement cost was priced, and that a receipt or a connected account can verify it another way.
- A saved inventory opens through a loading portal, and the list starts loading it as soon as the row is touched. A line can be hidden from the report with a minus, or deleted from inside the line. Hidden lines move to the bottom and can be filtered. A line that would be firmer with a receipt shows an attention mark. The owner can declare the line, describe it, and attach a picture or PDF.
- After the first inventory is saved, My inventory opens on the list and New inventory. The four how-to steps stay only until that first scan.
- A walk prices lines in batches instead of one search per clip, and the live scanner stops after repeated failures. A line no longer keeps a thumbnail from a second it shares with another object. The end-of-scan check can fill an empty brand, model, serial, color, or description from the clip and what was said, then that line is priced again.
- The Information page states that 100proof is a verifiable personal property inventory and a commercial property inventory, for insurance, a move, or a contents claim. New questions cover moving first and a home inventory near you. Menu and page links load with a normal page request so a tap still works on a phone.
- A 2,600 sq ft house study records walk time, price accuracy before any receipt, and whether items over $250 were captured. Jewelry is excluded. A new-user time is not stated until three walks are logged (`docs/HOUSE_STUDY.md`).
- Each line has one confidence number, and the chip, the proof ledger, the evidence panel, the assessed total, and the claim file all read it. A formal appraisal with its document attached is 99 and its amount is the value; a typed appraisal amount without the document counts as a stated price. A receipt that names the item, photographed, filmed during the scan, or matched in Connections, is 99. A model number read from a label close-up, or named the same by both reads, is 95 when priced as that exact product. A confirmed model name is 90. A brand and a matching model is 85; over $250 that needs the two reads to agree or a close-up, so a model guessed by the end-of-scan text check no longer counts there. An ordinary item under $250 priced from a store page is 75. A brand alone over $250 is 65, and nothing identifying over $250 is 40. A price no page backs is held at 60, an open count at 70, and two versions, two reads that disagree, or a comparable outside half to double the stated price at 50; that last one asks the owner which is right. A line counts at 75 under $250 and 85 above. A line stated and not seen has no percentage. "Model number" now means a code such as CGS700P2MS, not any digit: "65-inch", "12-cup", "4K", and a year are not model numbers. A self-declared value is shown in red and never replaces the scan price.
- A stated price is sent with each line to the price search, and the comparable is kept as found. The old rule that clamped a comparable to four times the stated price, a number no page showed, is gone. A later label, photo, or receipt check can no longer overwrite a documented appraisal.
- Actual cash value is set only when the age is known. Without it, the line says so and the summary counts those lines instead of showing cash value equal to replacement. A line has a Year bought field, and a label photo that reads a year also sets it. Damage still lowers cash value only, never replacement. Kinds with no wear schedule (jewelry, art, collectibles) keep replacement less any damage.
- Scans run up to 15 minutes in 30-second clips. After Done, My inventory opens at once and the new card reads "Scan will be completed soon" while the stills upload and up to six label stills are checked for over-$250 lines with no model.
- Drinks, pens, notepads, trash, food, and empty shipping boxes are not listed. Storage boxes are grouped by size. Decor is one row per piece, shown together under Decor. Plates, glasses, flatware, cookware, towels, and sheets are one bulk line per kind, named "Various …", with an estimated count and a per-piece price; sterling silverware stays on its own row.
- My inventory: a homeowner can delete an inventory with its item events and evidence files (`cost_ledger` is kept); a loading screen holds until the list is known; "+ New" sits on the header row; the card cover is the highest-priced item and the card shows the date and item count; cards have depth, a red trash control with a smooth press, and the Delete button shows three dots while it works. On a first visit the four steps sit on one row, the tapped step opens its detail attached to it, and one button starts the first inventory.
- Starting a scan is a short wizard: the inventory name, then the country, then the address, each asked in the same place, with a Back arrow and the answers collected above. The address is verified in place (green check, "Location verified"). Begin scan opens the camera at once; the second Begin scan button is gone. Filming is a full-screen camera view with the time left, the clip count, a record/pause control, Done, a front/back switch, and zoom when the camera supports it. The inventory's name and address are not shown while filming. A pause can ask for a label or receipt on the same screen.
- The README, app and worker READMEs, package and infra READMEs, judging map, GTM, eval checklist, carrier evidence note, and ADR notes were brought up to date with the build (15-minute scans, room chips, two Meta models by version, the five-run rule). Placeholder folders now say they are placeholders.

### Added
- A research layer for scan-to-scan metrics, kept apart from the app (`docs/research/README.md`). The camera keeps counters for each scan on the inventory document: clips, lines listed before merging, merges, repeats asked and answered, photo-check verdicts, room prompts, and reminders. They are never shown or exported. `apps/web/research/` turns a scored run into one metrics record: match score, capture, precision, duplicate rate before and after, hand-list brands read, read-twice agreement, photo-check counts, Not-here rate, room prompts per clip, ledger shares, and the Reminders setting. `metrics add`, `metrics compare`, and `metrics summary` keep `docs/research/scan-metrics.json`, the trend in `SCAN_METRICS_TREND.md`, and demo cards per feature beat in `DEMO_COMPARE.md`. Each card is the same walk without and with the step, never a claim about another product. The harness fails if the app imports the research layer, or if a counter reaches the claim file or the CSV.
- Proof ledger. The top of the list splits the total into four bands: receipt or appraisal, seen and counted, seen but not counted yet, and stated but not seen. It says "Of your $X, $Y is counted: a receipt or appraisal backs it, or what the camera saw meets the bar for its price." The bands follow each line's confidence number, so the counted amount equals the assessed value. Hidden lines are left out. The screen and the claim file say that proven describes the evidence on each line, not what a policy pays. The claim file carries the ledger, and the CSV has an `evidence_band` column for each line. CSV clip rows are named by the rooms tapped in them.
- Tap to replay. Each line plays the seconds it was filmed: from a second before its clearest second, for about three and a half seconds, inside the clip, with what was said in those seconds captioned underneath. A "Stated, not seen" line plays where it was said.
- Before-and-after walk. A Scan more walk is compared object by object with the saved lines. Each saved line is seen again, moved, seen with damage not seen before, a possible match, not seen in this walk, or not checked because its room was not filmed. The inventory shows the counts and the lines not seen, and each line shows a chip such as "Not seen since November 4". It never says missing or stolen.
- What-belongs-here prompts. On a pause, once per room, Muse Spark 1.3 gets the room name and its listed lines and returns up to three questions about things a room like that usually has that were not filmed, each with a reason and no brand. "Not here" is remembered. Nothing says the list is complete, and a reply that claims it is dropped. These calls are their own `belongs` call type in `cost_ledger` (migration `20261104220000_cost_ledger_belongs_call.sql`, applied to 100proof-dev), shown in Measures as "Room reminders".
- Read twice. Every line that could be over $250 is identified twice by Muse Spark 1.3, from two different stills, under a typed contract (kind, brand, model, and the visible evidence). Neither read sees the other's answer or the line's name. Reads that agree mark the line "Read twice", and a brand both gave fills an empty brand, logged as the check. Agreement never settles the model by itself. Reads that disagree mark the line "Two reads disagree" and keep both side by side. The line is held below 90, out of the assessed total, until the owner picks read 1, read 2, or neither. The top of the list says how many lines were read twice, how many agreed, and how many are unsure. Up to 15 lines a scan.
- One object, one line, across rooms. At Done, every pair of lines from different clips is compared on identity, in any room: serial, model, brand, the kind of thing (a TV stand and a TV console are one kind, as are a dresser and a wardrobe cabinet), color, and the words that describe it. Different serials, models, brands, or colors are never merged, and neither are two lines from one clip or a listing's numbered twins ("Vase", "Vase 2"). A strong match merges: the line with more evidence is kept, with the larger count, its photo or the other's, and "Also seen in …" when the rooms differ. A weaker match, or any decor or kind homes often have several of, is not merged. It is saved on the inventory as a possible repeat, shown side by side with "Same item" or "Two items". Run on test 1A's saved lines, this merges the TV console, the TV, the dresser, and the foot massager, and asks about seven pairs.
- Repeats are found while filming, not only at Done. After each clip, a sure match is merged at once and a weaker one waits; the camera says how many, and a pause shows them one at a time with both stills side by side. Before a weaker pair is asked, Muse Spark 1.3 compares the two photos under a typed contract (same, different, or unsure, with a reason and the visible cues) and its answer is shown above "Same item" and "Two items". It is advice: it never merges or keeps lines by itself. A question saved for later gets the same comparison the first time the inventory shows it. A Scan more walk is matched against the lines already saved: a sure match joins the saved line after the save, a weaker one is asked. Up to 12 photo comparisons run per scan.
- Room chips on the camera: Front entrance, Primary bedroom, Hallway, and Gym, plus More (Bedrooms 2 to 5, Bathrooms 1 to 3, and Add a room). A tap sets the room from that second of the clip on, and the next clip starts in it. Each clip stores its taps. A line takes the room tapped at the second it was seen, so lines land in the right room from the clip that found them. Without a tap, "Walkthrough" is no longer a room: an unclear room is "Room not set", a closet folds into the room it opens from, and clear names such as "Entry" map to the seed room. The listing is told the tapped rooms and is told never to put a room in an item name. When the listing sees a different room than the one tapped, the camera asks: "Switch to Gym" moves that clip's later lines and keeps filming there, "Keep" leaves them, and the same room is not asked again until the next tap. Until a room is tapped, the camera says "Tap the room you are in". Names lose a room prefix ("Walkthrough: wall mirror" is "Wall mirror") and placeholder words ("Unspecified size not clear clogs" is "Clogs"), on new lines and on saved lines when they open. One object listed under two rooms in the same clip is one line, not a count of two. Clips in the video list are named by the rooms tapped in them, such as "Clip 3 · Hallway, Gym".
- A walkthrough scorecard (`docs/SCORECARD.md`, `apps/web/lib/scorecard.ts`). On 2026-11-03 a small personal space was inventoried by hand (40 entries, 60 units, four rooms) as the reference a scan is scored against: one credited match per expected unit, every extra unit an error, correct exclusions disclosed but not scored, reconciliation required, and a pending decision withholds the score. Nine acceptance cases run in the harness. No run is scored yet.
- The scorecard has a Reminders checkbox for each run: the scorer ticks it when what-belongs-here reminders were on during that walk. It is kept with the run as `remindersOn`, and a five-run series cannot mix runs with reminders on and off. It changes nothing in the scan.
- A research series is five scored walks of that space. The accuracy rate is their average match score, and it is stated only after five scored runs. The target is 95 on every run and on the average. Sample variance above 4, a standard deviation of 2 points, marks the walks inconsistent: the average is recorded and not treated as stable. A pending or rejected run withholds the series. No series is recorded yet.
- Each clip's listing checks condition (new, good, fair, worn, damaged) and notes damage it can see with a severity and the second it is clearest. Damage lowers cash value (minor keeps 90%, moderate 70%, severe 40%, after wear); replacement stays the price of a new one. A later clip's worse damage is kept and the cash value recomputed. A photo or an answer can correct the condition.
- When no brand is read or said, the listing may name a likely brand with its clues. It is shown as "Likely …, not confirmed", priced as a comparable, and cleared by a label or receipt brand.
- The price batch checks each comparable against the line's kind, size, material, features, and brand. "Exact:" is downgraded to "Comparable:" unless the brand or model is confirmed.
- The check at Done runs on every scan and also fixes categories, only to a listed category, logged as changed by the check.
- New invariants (set 2): damage not reflected in cash value (blocker), damaged without a note, exact without a confirmed identity, likely brand marked settled, unknown category. The claim file is schema 2, with condition, damage, and likely brand per line; the CSV adds the same three columns.
- Information explains condition and damage in the steps and the example line, and answers "How does 100proof handle damage or wear?" and "What if I do not know the brand?".

### Fixed
- Joining two listings of the same object keeps the web-checked price when the other line only has an estimate from the video. A later line with the same description inherits that checked price, and its own age and damage set the cash value. "No damage" is not stored as a damage note.
- A useful life of 0, or an empty model option copied from the example, no longer drops the whole clip. A placeholder "none" is not stored as damage, and a price or price paid of 0 is not stored. A template age of 0 no longer hides the year the person said.
- A year heard in a later clip of the same object lowers cash value while filming, and that change is logged. Settling the brand on a damaged line recomputes cash value instead of copying the full price. A web price on a damaged line uses the line's own useful life.
- "Undamaged" and "not new" are no longer read as damaged or new. A likely brand no longer marks the model as settled.
- A category correction recomputes cash value on the new kind's useful life. Joining the same object keeps an age or worse damage from the other clip and recomputes cash value. The useful life stays on the line.
- A grouped line under $100 does not take one piece's damage off the group price.
