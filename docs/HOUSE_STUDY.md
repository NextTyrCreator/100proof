# House study: 2,600 sq ft, front yard, and back yard

The same walk, repeated. Each run is one person filming one 2,600 sq ft house and both yards, then a reviewer scoring the inventory against the video. Nothing here is a public accuracy claim. A number goes on the site only after it is in this log and Steven approves the wording.

The scorer is `apps/web/lib/house-study.ts`. The harness checks its rules. Jewelry is left out of the over-$250 check, as Steven asked.

This study measures time and prices on a full house. Whether the scan puts the right units on the list is measured separately, against a hand-walked reference of one small space: `docs/SCORECARD.md`.

## What a hand inventory takes

No controlled study was found that timed a person itemizing a 2,600 sq ft house plus both yards. What is published:

| Source | What they timed | Their figure | How to read it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Virtual Home Inventory](https://virtualhomeinventory.com/what-to-expect/) | A professional on site. A 2,500 sq ft house is their example. | 3 to 4 hours on site. The file is ready in about 10 business days. High-value photos add time. | Closest size. It is a paid service, not someone doing it themselves. |
| [Intellist](https://intellist.app/blog/home-inventory-for-insurance-guide) | A "typical home," one room a day. | 2 to 4 hours in all, or 20 to 30 minutes a room across a week. | Vendor guide. Not timed on a 2,600 sq ft house. |
| [SaveOr](https://www.saveor.com/blog/how-to-create-home-inventory-for-insurance) | An app-assisted list, size not stated. | 90 to 120 minutes. | Vendor guide aimed at finishing in under two hours. |
| [VaultTag](https://www.vaulttag.com/blog/how-to-document-your-home-in-under-an-hour) | A lighter photo pass of a 3-bedroom home. | About 1 hour. | Not a full itemized list. |
| [HomyScan](https://homyscan.com/solutions/home-inventory-app/) | Photos, then values later. | About 10 minutes a room. A house takes a weekend of spare moments. | Vendor. |

Until this log has three finished walks, 100proof does not state how long a new person should take. After three, the new-user figure is the average of the hands-on minutes below.

## One walk

1. The house is about 2,600 sq ft of living area, with a front yard and a back yard. Record the real square footage if it differs.
2. Before filming, do not make the truth list from memory. Film first.
3. Start a stopwatch when the person taps to film. Stop it when they would hand the file over, before any receipt is added and before Connections. Write two times: hands-on minutes (filming and answering) and waiting minutes (the app working).
4. Film every room and both yards. Say the room, the brand, and the year. Use Scan more if the first 15 minutes are not enough. Note if the cap stopped the walk.
5. Download the claim file before any receipt or Connections match. That file is the before-connections record.
6. Watch the video once. Write the truth list from the video, not from the inventory: each thing a person would claim, the count, the brand and model when visible or spoken, and a replacement price for one unit. Mark anything the video does not actually show.
7. Answer the comparison questions below. Then score the file with `scoreHouseWalk` (or fill the row by hand using the same rules).

## The metrics

| Metric | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Hands-on minutes | Stopwatch. Filming and answering only. |
| Waiting minutes | Stopwatch, while a review or a price is running. |
| Filmed minutes | Sum of the clips. The scorer reads this. |
| Hit the cap | Yes if Scan more was required, or the walk stopped because the limit did. |
| Lines | Lines on the before-connections file. |
| Missing | On the truth list, visible in the video, absent from the file. |
| Should not be there | On the file, but the video does not show it. A duplicate of one object counts. |
| Price, before connections | Replacement on a line with no receipt and no Connections match, against the reviewer's price. Within 25% counts. A line with a receipt is left out of this column. |
| Over $250 recovered | Truth unit price over $250, not jewelry. Recovered only if the file names it and ties it to a moment in a clip. Watches still count. |
| New-user minutes | Blank until three walks. Then the average of hands-on minutes. |

Superiority is the comparison of this table with the hand-inventory times above, after the walks exist. It is not assumed.

## Comparison questions, every walk

Answer from the video first, then check the file. Write yes, no, or a count.

1. Did the file name every room the video entered, including the front yard and the back yard?
2. Which objects worth more than $250, other than jewelry, are in the video and missing from the file?
3. Which lines are on the file even though the video does not show that object?
4. For each priced line that has no receipt: is the replacement within 25% of a price you would defend from the video?
5. Did a later clip's damage, year, or brand land on the same line, or did it become a second line?
6. Did the walk stop because of the time cap before both yards were filmed?
7. What one change to the product would have recovered a missing object or removed a false one?
