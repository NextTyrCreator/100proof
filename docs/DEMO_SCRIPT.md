# Final demo video: words and storyboard

Target length: 2:59. Spoken copy is about 380 words, paced at roughly 130 words per minute so it sounds calm rather than rushed. The first six seconds are silent.

This script is written for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon rubric:

- **Impact, 30%:** the fire hook, the documented insurance problem, and the close.
- **Use of Meta AI tooling, 25%:** Muse Spark 1.3 and Muse Voice Transcribe 1.0 do visible, necessary work.
- **Demo, 25%:** one simple path—walk, inventory, strengthen a line, download.
- **Technical depth, 20%:** independent reads, typed model-output checks, evidence links, call logging, and the source hash.

The theme is **Build for Your World**. The story is not a feature list. It is one transformation: a phone walk becomes a contents file before the owner needs it.

## Thumbnail

Use the burning-house frame with one short creator-style hook in big white type:

- **Option 1 (Best all-around):** `WHAT DID YOU OWN?`
- **Option 2 (Viewer challenge):** `NAME EVERY ITEM.`
- **Option 3 (Curiosity + payout gap):** `75% PAID. PROVE THE REST.` *(Use only because the opening immediately explains the 2025 LA wildfire advance rule.)*

**Layout**

- Crop to 16:9 and keep the burning house large enough to read on a phone.
- Put the house on one side and the headline in the cleanest dark area on the other.
- Set the headline on two stacked lines in bold system sans (`#FFFFFF`) with a dark navy shadow (`#0B1F3A`).
- Put the unmodified 100proof logo in the opposite lower corner with clear space around it.
- Keep teal out of the fire graphic. Teal means verified and belongs later on the receipt-verified result.

## Edit rhythm and shareability

- Change the visual every six to ten seconds, but keep one continuous story.
- Put the surprising `75%` fact in the first frame, then explain its exact meaning immediately.
- Show the product by 0:48. Do not spend the first minute pitching.
- Use captions for every spoken word; many judges and social viewers watch muted.
- Keep text cards to one idea and no more than two short lines.
- Use the same phrase three times through the story: **the list**, **the evidence**, **before anything happens**.
- Let one uncertainty remain visible. Honest uncertainty is more memorable than a perfect-looking fake result.
- Remove loading and model wait time, but do not imply the processing was instant. A small `Processing shortened for video` caption is enough.
- Use one restrained music rise at the product reveal and one at the download. The fire does not need cinematic sound design.

## Claims that must remain exact

- The 75%, 80%, and 100% figures are advances reported after the 2025 Los Angeles wildfires, not normal payout percentages and not a promise for any policy.
- A policyholder could still recover up to the full value later by completing the required inventory, subject to the policy limit and documentation.
- Actual cash value considers age and wear. Replacement cost is like kind and quality. The carrier decides coverage and payment.
- The product calls two Meta models: Muse Spark 1.3 and Muse Voice Transcribe 1.0.
- `Read twice` requires a higher-value line and two usable views. It is a check, not verification.
- Camera-seen lines link to a clip and second. Spoken-only lines are marked `Stated, not seen`.
- A receipt reaches `Receipt verified` only after a real match is applied.
- The source hash covers clip content hashes and scan metadata, not the itemized line text.
- No scored Run 01 exists yet. Do not show or speak an accuracy, match, capture, or precision number.
